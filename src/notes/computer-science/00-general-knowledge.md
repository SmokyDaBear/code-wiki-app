# Computer Science General Knowledge

This section contains general knowledge notes about computer science, covering fundamental concepts, theories, and principles that form the basis of the field. Topics include algorithms, data structures, computational theory, computer architecture, networking basics, and more.

## Overview

Computers are electronic devices that process data according to a set of instructions called programs. They consist of hardware components (physical parts) and software components (programs and operating systems).

### Key Concepts

- **Algorithm**: A step-by-step procedure or formula for solving a problem.
- **Data Structure**: A way of organizing and storing data to enable efficient access and modification.
- **Computational Theory**: The study of the capabilities and limitations of computers.
- **Computer Architecture**: The design and organization of a computer's components, including the CPU, memory, and input/output devices.
- **Networking**: The practice of connecting computers to share resources and information.

### Important Topics

- **Binary System**: The fundamental language of computers, using only two digits (0 and 1) to represent data.
- **Operating Systems**: Software that manages computer hardware and software resources and provides common services for computer programs.
- **Programming Languages**: Formal languages used to write computer programs (e.g., Python, Java, C++).
- **Databases**: Organized collections of data that can be easily accessed, managed, and updated.
- **Cybersecurity**: The practice of protecting computer systems and networks from digital attacks.
